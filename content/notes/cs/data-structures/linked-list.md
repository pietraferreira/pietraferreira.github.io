# Linked List
---
It maintains the insertion order of elements, however they are **not** indexed. When searching, we start with the head and work our way through.

The complexity ([[big-o-notation|big-O]]) of finding a value is of **O(n)**. The best and worst of insertion/deletion are **0(1)** for best and **O(n)** for worst.
